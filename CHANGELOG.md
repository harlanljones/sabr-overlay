## [0.2.0] - 2026-10-23

### Added
- Six panels: Situation, Matchup, Win Probability, At-Bat Pitches, Pitcher Today, Batted Balls.
- Baseball Savant percentile bubbles with estimated fallbacks.
- Broadcast delay sync via "click the pitch you just saw".
- Draggable, minimizable panels with persisted layout; `Alt+Shift+S` toggle.
- Background fetch proxy to bypass page CSP.

### Changed
- Renamed from "Sabermetrics Overlay" to **SABR Overlay**. Settings key moved to `sabrOverlay.v1`; previous layouts reset.
